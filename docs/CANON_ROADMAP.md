## 4. Devils (V) and a naming problem to fix
Canon devils named in the sources: **Megicula** (Vanica), **Bete** (Zenon), **Lucifero** (Dante), **Zagred** (Lucius); Liebe (Asta), Gimodelo and Slotos (Nacht's).
The mod has Megicula ✅, Zagred ✅, and a devil called **Lucifugus**: I found no canon devil with that name. Consider renaming it **Lucifero** (the final devil, a big boss) or Bete.

## 7. FTB Quests plan

**What the mod already has.** A "Clover Magic" advancement tab with 24 quests (hidden advancements fired by the code, e.g. `got_grimoire`, `talked_orsi`, `level_5`, `devil_union`, `joined_squad`), and `extras/ftbquests/clover_magic.snbt` with 23 of them as FTB tasks (all `advancement` tasks with XP rewards). The mod runs without FTB Quests.

**What FTB Quests adds.** Chapters with maps, dependencies, locked chapters, several task types, and rewards. Proposed use:

| Need | FTB Quests feature |
|---|---|
| "Reach level 5/10/15/20", "got a grimoire", "joined a squad", "talked to X" | `advancement` task on the mod's advancements (already works) |
| "Kill 10 Midnight Sun mages" | `kill` task (entity `clovermagic:midnight_mage`) |
| "Craft a Devil's Contract", "Bring 5 Mana Crystals (magic stones)" | `item` task (consume or just check) |
| "Find the Dungeon / the Church / the Tower" | `location` / `structure` task |
| "Rewards" | item, xp, loot table rewards, or a command reward (the server runs it) |
| Order of the story | one chapter per arc, locked until the previous chapter is done |
| Squads | FTB Teams could map to the mod's squads (optional, later) |

**Draft chapters (one per arc). Tasks marked ⬜ need new content from section 1-3.**
1. *Entrance Exam*: get a grimoire; talk to Orsi; talk to Asta; first mission; hunt boars; reach level 5. ✅ all exist.
2. *The Dungeon*: find the Dungeon; cross the trap corridor (location); mine the sealed door; defeat the guardian (spider ✅ / Mars ⬜); bring back 5 magic stones (Mana Crystals).
3. *Capital Assault*: kill 10 Midnight Sun mages; defend a village raid ⬜; meet Julius ✅.
4. *The Eye of the Midnight Sun*: find the camp 🟡; free 3 captured villagers ⬜.
5. *The Seabed Temple*: find the temple ⬜; get the Magic Stone ⬜; defeat Vetto ⬜.
6. *Witches' Forest*: find the Witch Queen ⬜; cure the Cursed Arms ⬜; join the Star Awards ⬜.
7. *Royal Knights*: join a squad ✅; earn squad points ✅; reach squad rank ⬜.
8. *Elf Reincarnation*: collect 10 magic stones; defeat Patry ⬜.
9. *Heart Kingdom*: train with Nacht ⬜; Devil Union ✅; unlock Mana Zone ⬜.
10. *Spade Kingdom*: craft a Devil's Contract ✅; make a deal (second grimoire / fifth leaf ✅); kill Zagred ✅; Lucifero ⬜.
11. *The Wizard King*: reach level 20 ✅; final trial ⬜.

**Caveat.** FTB Quests could not be run while writing the shipped file, so its format may need a small fix. To generate all eleven chapters correctly, export one real chapter from your FTB Quests install (with a kill task, an item task and a location task) and send it, so I can match the exact format of your version.
